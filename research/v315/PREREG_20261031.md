# V315 prospective screen: phase-aware RF-port relief by selective recomputation

Frozen before holdout execution on 2026-10-31. This is a **capacity-changing hypothesis**, not a scheduling-only change. It remains a research screen until measured physical datapath, correct register values, timing, power and GPU traces exist.

## Mechanism
A compiler marks a subset of hot-bank operand reads as exactly rematerializable. An issue-side controller may recompute an operand on an otherwise spare ALU rather than read it from a saturated register-file bank. This trades RF read bandwidth for ALU bandwidth. It is **not** assumed to be a novel technique. In particular, the control must preserve operand values, dependencies, side effects and exact physical-register identity. No actual instruction semantics are implemented in this screen.

Four issue slots, two hot-bank read ports/cycle, four ALU operations/cycle, two operands/instruction, rematerializable fraction q=0.65 of hot-bank reads, one extra ALU operation per selected operand. Other-bank demand, writeback, RF timing and area are not modeled; their omission makes results optimistic.

For mean hot-bank demand d reads/instruction and selected fraction f of eligible hot-bank reads:
  lambda(d,f) = min(4, 2/[d(1-q f)], 4/[1+d q f]).
The closed-form crossover is f*(d) = clip((4d-2)/[q d (2+4)], 0,1), where clip is saturation. This is an **optimistic fluid resource envelope**, not achieved IPC.

## Frozen experiments (equal numbers of jobs in two phases)
- predicted-win: d=(1.6,0.6), seed 315801, 100000 jobs per phase. Predicted dynamic vs globally optimized static recomputation = 1.07448x; acceptance interval 1.06..1.09.
- predicted-no-win: d=(1.8,1.8), seed 315802, 100000 jobs per phase. Predicted gain ~1.000x; acceptance 0.995..1.005.
- prospective unseen transfer: d=(1.4,0.7), seed 315803, 100000 jobs per phase. Predicted 1.06115x; acceptance 1.045..1.075.
- adverse sensitivity: q=0.2 and recompute cost c=2 (derive new crossover); no positive claim frozen.

Static baseline: select one f from grid {0,0.001,...,1} minimizing the expected total resource-envelope cycles over both phases. For additional strong control, report hindsight-optimized static f on sampled jobs. Candidate: phase-aware f*(d), fixed before sampling. **Most important equal-information control:** a compiler that can specialize rematerialization by phase with the same phase labels, eligibility and ALU budget. It can reproduce candidate decisions exactly; any incremental advantage over this strong control must be demonstrated, not assumed.

## Gates
1. Independently sampled hot-bank read demand must agree with the precommitted law in the frozen intervals.
2. Strong phase-aware control must be beaten by >=2% on a non-tautological mechanism before V315 can advance to RTL. If the control is exactly equivalent, retire V315 as an architectural novelty claim.
3. Physical gate, if ever reached: complete value-correct RTL, matching compute resources, timing/electrical/area/power and realistic traces. A fluid gain is not sufficient.

Prior-art anchors: Malekeh (2023) https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17501; LTRF (2020) https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.09330; CORF (2019) https://doi.org/10.1145/3352460.3358318 (verify bibliographic mapping before citing DOI). These cover RF caching, compiler-assisted RF transformations and coalescing; they do not establish novelty for this mechanism.
