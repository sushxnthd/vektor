# Vektor-058 preregistered predictions

P1: FIFO ordered saturated ages make first-urgent and maximum-age urgent equivalent.
P2: Queue waiting bound is threshold plus capacity plus output-stall cycles.
P3: Modulo-age wrap may invalidate the ordered-age invariant.
P4: Two independent queue models agree on tagged response accounting.
P5: Completion-first arbitration without an age guard may delay older requests indefinitely.
P6: Differential RTL and generic synthesis are expected to pass but have not been executed.

These are component-level hypotheses, not chip-level performance claims.
