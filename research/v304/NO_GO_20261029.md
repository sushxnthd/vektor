2026-10-29. Frozen protocol: commit `6a0d6c78290bc083d10e736b07cf50c27409c733`. Candidate: `bank=(reg+2*warp)%16`; strongest equal-state control: `bank=(reg XOR (2*warp))%16`. Uncolored `reg%16` is **not** a strong control.

## Exact negative predictive law

Let `S_w` be the set of banks needed by a warp's next instruction. For a group `G`, simultaneous single-cycle collection is feasible iff `max_b sum_(w in G) 1[b in S_w] <= P`, where `P` is the number of read ports per bank. Distinct operands to the same bank are separate requests and may be collected across cycles.

For H1, the source pair is `(0,1)`. Since `2w` is even,
`(0 XOR 2w, 1 XOR 2w) = (2w mod 16, (2w+1) mod 16)`,
exactly the same bank pair as additive rotation. Therefore the candidate and strong XOR control have identical bank-demand sets on every H1 instruction. **No bank-admission throughput advantage is possible for this regime in the frozen model**, regardless of seed. The observed 0% gain is predicted by exact bank-set equivalence rather than statistical coincidence.

## Prior art and physical limits

GPGPU-Sim already exposes warp-ID-aware register banking, including a conventional `(register_number + warp_id) % num_banks` mapping. See https://github.com/gpgpu-sim/gpgpu-sim_distribution and its operand-collector register-bank code. CORF (ASPLOS 2019) explicitly discusses GPU register bank assignment and the tradeoff between conflicts and coalescing: https://www.cs.ucr.edu/~nael/pubs/asplos19.pdf . LTRF includes compile-time register renumbering to reduce register-bank conflicts: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.09330 . Additive warp coloring is therefore not a defensible novelty claim.

The model does not include physical register layout, clocked RTL, wire delays, bank decoder area, compiler allocation, write ports, occupancy effects of spills, cache-hit bypasses, or real GPU instruction mixes. No speedup, timing, power or RTX-5090-equivalence claim is made.

## Decision

**NO-GO. Retire V304 without RTL or synthesis**: frozen strong-control H1 gate failed and the core mechanism has direct prior art. Do not retune after opening the holdouts. Preserve all negative evidence in this unmerged branch; choose a genuinely different, bottleneck-grounded mechanism in a later cycle.
