# V270 training sensitivity (E1; no holdouts opened)
Independent analytic extension: for each of two operand collectors ready with probability q, compatible opcode probability c, and lane-count distribution with pair eligibility P_m, the issue-only expected rate is T_m=r_m[(2q-q^2)+q^2*c*P_m]. This assumes independent collector readiness, independent lane masks and an offered pair; it does not model fetch, register ports or latency. A 36-point training sweep over lane activation u in {0.2,0.4,0.6,0.8}, q in {0.35,0.7,1}, c in {0.25,0.75,1} gives at most 1.061x adaptive/static ideal rate before clock derating. Full compaction may demand up to 1.058x higher adaptive clock to be beaten on modeled issue rate. These are analytical model outputs, not RTL throughput or physical timing. Frozen V270 unseen holdouts remain unopened. The positive 6+2 construction is therefore a boundary case, not representative of generic SIMT workloads.
