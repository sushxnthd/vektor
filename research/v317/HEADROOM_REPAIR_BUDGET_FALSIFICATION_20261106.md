Any candidate-only repair beyond either budget kills even this idealized density advantage. If the baseline also requires repair, or both clocks change, rerun the formula with *both* repaired measured values. Extra common tile overhead, bandwidth limits, occupancy loss, increased cycles and power can change the boundary; none is measured here.
