## Candidate implementation (fully specified)
Four FIFO slots in oldest-to-newest order, four banks, two read ports per bank, two operands per request, at most one service per slot per cycle. State `phase` is one bit, reset 0. Each cycle select a feasible subset using the same greedy feasibility predicate as compact greedy but scan slot order `[0,1,2,3]` when phase=0 and `[0,2,1,3]` when phase=1. The oldest slot is always evaluated first; no slot is selected twice. Toggle phase each cycle, even if the FIFO is empty. Dequeue selected slots simultaneously, preserving FIFO order of survivors; then enqueue arrivals until capacity four. Do not add a search oracle or additional state. The two alternate scans, one flip-flop, clock/reset load, and selection mux are all charged to the candidate. An implementer may reject the candidate immediately if this state cannot meet exact equivalence and fairness invariants.

## Predictive boundary (necessary, not sufficient)
Let `s` be candidate/compact throughput at identical clock and resource, `f` the candidate/compact achieved clock ratio, `o` the candidate/compact effective occupancy ratio, and `b` the sustainable bandwidth-limited ratio. Then an optimistic upper envelope is `G <= min(s*f*o,b)`; a 2% end-to-end improvement requires **both** `s*f*o >= 1.02` and `b >= 1.02`. Any candidate with `s <= 1.02` has effectively no physical headroom if `f*o < 1`. These are screening inequalities, not a demonstrated throughput model; test the bound on unseen traffic and reject if it fails calibration.

Development seeds: 20261121–20261124 (existing, not holdout). **Unseen seeds**: 20261201–20261216, never used for tuning. Independent checker must implement a separate per-cycle reference, prove conservation `offered=admitted+dropped`, `admitted=completed+remaining` over full runs (including warmup), FIFO survivor ordering, two-port bank feasibility, no duplicate grants, and oldest-request eligibility when its bank operands fit. In a full queue, do not silently overwrite arrivals.

## Decision gate
Primary paired metric: geometric mean of candidate/compact throughput over all 16 holdout seeds × all regimes × all rates, with separate burst strata. Require >=1.02x overall **and** >=1.02x on at least one prespecified stratum, no >1% regression on any prespecified stratum, no increased starvation, and a bootstrap 95% lower confidence bound >1.00 on the seed-level paired log ratio. The bootstrap is descriptive and cannot fix a biased traffic model. Do not retune on the holdout. Failure = **kill rotating-scan candidate**, preserve source and negative result, do not launch physical work.

If passed, proceed sequentially: independent behavioral equivalence of reference vs RTL, simulator and lint, synthesis against same compact-greedy RTL, identical placement constraints, electrical-legal routing, matched timing/area, toggles/power proxies, realistic wire length/load and port sensitivity, workload throughput/traffic/occupancy, and a second unseen traffic-family transfer. Any electrical violation, >2% area/timing/occupancy penalty that destroys the boundary, or bandwidth bottleneck kills the mechanism. Do not claim SRAM-integrated GPU, GPU IPC, or RTX-5090 equivalence without measurements.
