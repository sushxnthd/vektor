Evidence class: analytical deduction from previously recorded **unplaced Nangate45 mapped-area** and **OpenSTA control-only** results. This is not a new timing measurement, not physical PPA, and not scientific novelty.

## Active architectural kernel
- **Question:** Does the two-stage cutset FP32 finisher deliver a robust equal-resource area-delay-throughput improvement over the buffered V151 control?
- **Frontier:** V155 passed 24,220 RTL comparisons for each candidate. V156 control has a parse-clean OpenSTA report, -2.93 ns slack at 10 ns. Cutset timing is invalid because its netlist produced a parser error; its apparent slack MUST NOT be used.
- **Measurable objective:** minimize unplaced cell area × maximum path delay at equal interface, library, synthesis tool version, constraints, and latency; then verify sustained throughput and dependency stalls in an integrated Wave32 tile.
- **Strongest internal equal-resource baseline:** buffered V151 mapped with the same Yosys 0.9 and Nangate45 corner. An open external FMA baseline (e.g. CVFPU) remains a required later comparison, not yet established.
- **Mechanism hypothesis:** an elastic accumulation/rounding cutset reduces critical-path delay sufficiently to compensate for area and pipeline-state overhead without reducing issue/response throughput.
- **Decisive falsification:** parse-clean full-netlist STA shows no area-delay improvement, or integrated dependency/response tests show a throughput loss exceeding timing gain, or the effect disappears under matched cross-toolchain mapping.
- **Publishable threshold (prospective, not attained):** >=10% area-delay improvement with robust library/tool/seed controls, validated on unseen workloads, independent checking and a novelty audit demonstrating more than conventional pipelining.
- **Architecture-relevance threshold (prospective):** >=10% timing improvement at <=5% mapped-area overhead and no sustained throughput regression, or >=10% area-delay improvement under equal latency/resource accounting.
- **Kill condition:** three substantive experiments without improvement or new boundary, or a verified equal-resource Pareto loss; pivot to response bandwidth / register-file scheduling and preserve all negative results.

## Exact conditional inequality
Yosys 0.9 mapped areas (library units): control = 7558.390; cutset = 7821.464.
Area ratio = 1.0348055604434276. Let D_c and D_p be parse-clean, same-corner, same-constraint delays.

Cutset improves area-delay product **if and only if**
`D_p / D_c < 7558.390 / 7821.464 = 0.9663651203917836`.
It needs >3.3634879608% delay improvement to overcome the mapped-area disadvantage.
For >=10% area-delay improvement, `D_p/D_c <= 0.8697286083526052`, requiring >=13.0271391647% delay improvement.

The control's *unplaced proxy* D_c = 10 - (-2.93) = 12.93 ns, conditional on the reported control path being the relevant maximum path under the frozen constraints. Thus:
- ADP break-even cutset delay <12.4951010 ns.
- >=10% ADP improvement cutset delay <=11.2455909 ns.
These are **prediction thresholds**, not observed cutset timing.
