# Matched-latency correction

Buffered V151 and V153 cutset both have two elastic stages. At the same clock they have identical cycle latency and maximum output rate. A dependency-chain cycle-count reversal is therefore not a prediction of this particular matched comparison. Only mapped clock period, hardware cost, and later system integration can differentiate them.

A one-stage V151 option has lower latency and may outperform a two-stage candidate on dependent workloads. That is a separate experiment.

No scientific novelty is established by retiming.
